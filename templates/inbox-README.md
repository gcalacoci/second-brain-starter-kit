# Inbox — Quick Capture

Drop anything here you want in the brain later. This is the first layer of the
three-layer system: Inbox → Processed Knowledge → Index.

## What to drop here

- Meeting notes you haven't cleaned up yet
- Article links or PDFs you want summarized
- Voice memo transcripts
- Rough ideas, one-liners, screenshots
- Slack threads you paste in

## How it works

1. Dump here first, don't organize.
2. Run `dream sequence` or weekly review, and the agent will move items from here
   into `raw/sources/` (untouched) and create the processed pages in `raw/pages/`.
3. Processed entries are removed from inbox after ingestion.

## Daily habit

Dump everything to inbox. Organize once a week.
